# E2E-20: Safety Officer rejects (SPM-130, SPM-131, SPM-48)

Cross-role end-to-end test (DRAFT, not executed). Priority P1, blocked on SPM-168 question 2. The Safety Officer rejects an event's safety arrangement with a reason; the Coordinator is notified with the reason and the event cannot proceed. The outcome after rejection (terminal or revisable) is not defined by any story the product owner has confirmed, so this case proceeds on the position SPM-131 records. It crosses Safety Officer, Coordinator and Organiser, and if the answer is "revisable" it largely duplicates E2E-19.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. SPM-168 Q2 (Safety Officer rejection: terminal or revisable?) gates this case. The rejection path exists in code (the safety officer service requires a reason and stores it as a feedback item), so this is an undefined outcome, not a missing feature. Assumption `[S1]`: revisable, per the story owner's position in SPM-131 (returns to planning, blocks until a new approval, reviews all arrangements, does not cancel; "confirm with the PO"). The case does not describe the event as terminal. It may run before the answer arrives, with step 10 or 11 selected by the constant.
2. E2E-20 is P1; this open question does not gate a P0.
3. If the answer is "revisable", steps 1 to 9 duplicate the changes-requested path of E2E-19 apart from the rejected decision value and the "all arrangements" scope; E2E-20 and E2E-19 should then merge into one case with a reject variant, and E2E-20 retired. If the answer is "terminal", step 11 must be rewritten around a terminal state that no story names yet.
4. U7: no AC says the Organiser is notified of a Safety Officer rejection; the plan says "the EC (and EO, if your customer says so)". The case proceeds on "not notified" and asserts the absence (SPM-168 AC1). If the customer says the Organiser is told, step 9 changes.
5. The plan says the follow-up state "needs customer clarification"; the plan's EC notification with the reason is covered by SPM-131 AC1/AC2 (the Coordinator is told of the response and sees the decision and comments).
6. The "all arrangements marked for review" scope is SPM-131 AC5; the persisted form of "marked" is not fixed by an AC (compare U10). The event status after rejection (assumed Planning, not Rejected) has no AC; the event Rejected status belongs to the Coordinator's event-request review (SPM-83), not to the safety check.
7. Rejection reason length (SPM-168 Q3): the reason is valid under both candidate rules, so the case does not depend on that answer.
8. Builder gaps: no builders for booking requests, equipment requests or safety checks; the Confirm route body is unverified.
9. Existing Confluence page: none. Provenance: L76 (brief).
