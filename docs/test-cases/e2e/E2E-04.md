# E2E-04: Rejection (SPM-83, SPM-88, SPM-48, SPM-157)

Cross-role end-to-end test (executed in CI on 2026-11-06, PR #128). Covers an assigned Coordinator rejecting an event request with a reason, the Organiser being notified and seeing the reason, the status Rejected and the decision being kept in the history, and the negative that no further planning can start for the rejected event. It crosses Coordinator, Organiser, Lead and the booking and equipment features.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. No AC for the venue negative (brief U4). The plan says "no venue booking can be raised for the rejected event", but no AC forbids it. SPM-57 AC8 forbids equipment requests for a rejected event, and SPM-148 AC1 moves only Approved events to Planning. This asymmetry is recorded: step 10 (equipment) is backed by SPM-57 AC8; step 11 (venue) is backed only by the plan, which outranks the stories for expected results. [S1] The case proceeds on "a venue booking request for a rejected event is refused and creates no row". If the product accepts it, the case fails and the finding is a missing AC, not a test defect.
2. [S2] No AC says the Lead cannot reject. It follows from the Established facts in SPM-168 ("the assigned Coordinator approves or rejects; the Lead only assigns and reassigns") and SPM-123 AC10 (only the Lead assigns). Refusal code is ASSUMPTION.
3. [S3] Steps 12 and 13 (second decision refused; Organiser read-only on a rejected event) correspond to the lost-suite flows L38 and L38b, which SPM-168 lists as proposed additions to the plan, not plan content; no surviving AC states them. They are included as assumptions and may be removed if the team rejects the additions.
4. SPM-168 Q3 (rejection reason length: 1 to 2000, or 10 to 500 characters with at least 3 real words): the reason is five real words and 41 characters, valid under both. The length rule itself is not tested here. Resolved 2026-11-06: 10 to 500 characters, at least 3 real words (adopted from app enforcement). This case is unaffected — the reason satisfies both rules.
5. SPM-88 AC3 and AC4 both place the reason on the event detail page rather than in the notification text; the case asserts the page, not the notification body.
6. The Organiser is notified once; whether the Lead is told of a rejection has no story, so absence is asserted for the Lead (step 6).
7. Overlap: Confluence draft `E2E-EVENT-REJECT-01` (33620103). This case complements it with the negatives and the history; it does not supersede it. Brief provenance: R11; L36, L38, L38b; A3.5.
8. API status/error codes for refused calls are not fixed by any AC; refusal plus unchanged DB is asserted, the code marked ASSUMPTION.
