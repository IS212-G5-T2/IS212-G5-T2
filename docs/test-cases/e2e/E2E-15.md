# E2E-15: Main hall plus 3 breakout rooms (SPM-54, SPM-55, SPM-56, SPM-85, SPM-129)

Cross-role end-to-end test (DRAFT, not executed). Priority P1. An Event Coordinator requests four venues for one event; one room is lost to a competing event, Venue Staff approves three and rejects the conflicting one, the Coordinator cancels one approved room, and the Safety Officer then reviews the event. It crosses Coordinator, a second Coordinator, Venue Staff, Safety Officer and Organiser, and proves that each venue is decided and released independently, which no single-screen test can.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. U9 (brief section 9): the plan says "the safety check sees every venue". SPM-129 AC2/AC3 show the Safety Officer the venue capacity, layout, accessibility and the arrangements, but no AC says every venue of a multi-venue event is shown. Assumption: the Safety Officer sees every currently approved venue and no cancelled or rejected one. The step 15 assertion is plan-derived; the code lists approved bookings in the safety-officer queue detail, but the expected result is not taken from the code. Re-check once the story owner decides.
2. Plan versus story [C1]: the plan says a conflicting request is "flagged"; SPM-54 AC2 says the Coordinator cannot submit a conflicting request, and SPM-56 AC2 shows a conflict alert at approval time. The case covers both: the conflict arises after submission because a competing event is approved first (alert at VS approval), and a fresh submission is refused. The case does not claim a persisted flag (compare U10).
3. "The calendar links all rooms to the same event" has no AC. The case does not assert it; add a step once an AC or the venue calendar's behaviour is fixed.
4. Step 16 uses an assumed path for a flagged change (SPM-85 AC1/AC2 define what is flagged, not which actor or screen starts it). Which actor edits attendance is the same ambiguity as U6 on E2E-22. Drop the step if it is judged to belong to E2E-23.
5. Step 13 (last-booking warning) disturbs the state needed by steps 14 to 16; implement it as a separate test or on a second event.
6. Capacity numbers in step 10 assume "usable" capacity equals the venue capacity of approved bookings; SPM-54 AC11 does not define "usable" beyond that. The Pending capacity row is separate and should show 40 before step 7.
7. SPM-137 text was not read; the claim that booking decisions notify the Coordinator only comes from the brief (section 3, point 4). The absence assertions for other roles rest on that and on there being no story notifying them (SPM-168 AC1).
8. Builder gaps: no builder for booking requests or for venue_staff assignment to venues; the equipment confirmation endpoint name is read from `status.controller.ts`, its request body was not checked.
9. Existing Confluence page `E2E-VENUE-BOOKING-01` (33554524) overlaps the booking half only; this case complements it. Provenance: L60 covers a single booking cancel only; otherwise no surviving equivalent.
10. Automation finding (2026-11-06): step 14 is incomplete. A rejected venue request (Breakout 3) counts as unavailable and blocks the safety check request (422 SAFETY_PREREQUISITES_NOT_MET, "Pending venue confirmation to proceed.") until the Coordinator removes it (`DELETE /api/events/{id}/venue-requests/{requestId}`, allowed only for an unavailable request). Add step 14a: coordinator1 removes the rejected Breakout 3 request, then requests the safety check. Step 5: the conflict alert and 409 body list the BLOCKING booking's occupied window (the rival event's start minus setup to its end plus turnaround), as in E2E-09, not the main event's.
