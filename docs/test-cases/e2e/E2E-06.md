# E2E-06: Reassignment mid-planning (SPM-47, SPM-46, SPM-54, SPM-55, SPM-57, SPM-137, SPM-172, SPM-157, SPM-48)

Cross-role end-to-end test (DRAFT, not executed). Covers the Lead reassigning an event that has a pending venue booking request and equipment requests from coordinator1 to coordinator2: the old Coordinator loses access, the new one inherits the history, bookings and equipment requests, both are notified, and later decisions by Venue Staff and Technical Support notify the new Coordinator and not the old. It crosses Lead, two Coordinators, Venue Staff, Technical Support and Organiser, and the assignment, booking, equipment and notification features. There is no browser coverage of `/lead/reassign` today.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. Source conflict: SPM-47 AC1 lists the reassignable statuses as Submitted, Approved or Confirmed, with no Planning, while SPM-148 AC6 says Planning events count in the Lead's coordinator list and the plan has the reassignment happen mid-planning with a pending venue request (which is Planning by SPM-148 AC1). The plan outranks the stories, so the case assumes ([S2]) that a Planning event is listed and reassignable. If the product lists it as absent, that is a product defect or a story gap; raise it, do not change the step.
2. [S1] SPM-168 Q7 resolved 2026-11-06: organiser not notified on reassignment. SPM-47 AC8 names only both Coordinators. The case asserts the absence at step 9 as written.
3. [S3] No story notifies the Organiser of a booking decision (SPM-137 names the Coordinator only); absence asserted at step 11 on that basis.
4. [S4] The plan's "history, bookings and equipment requests intact" is partly AC-backed (SPM-47 AC5 covers clarifications, approval status and earlier notifications; SPM-157 AC7 covers history) and partly plan-only (bookings and equipment requests unchanged). The plan outranks; assertion retained and labelled.
5. [S5] The fixture making venue_staff1 the manager of `hall` is assumed available; builders do not obviously expose it. No builders exist for booking, equipment or clarification rows (created through the UI in pre-condition 4).
6. [S6] The wording of the reassigned notices is not fixed. The case asserts recipient, presence or absence of a link (SPM-77 AC6) and read state, not a sentence. API refusal codes are ASSUMPTION.
7. SPM-172 AC6 concerns equipment-decision notices only for decisions already made at reassignment time; the case seeds one such decision and checks it is removed from coordinator1 and not passed to coordinator2.
8. Reassignment to an unavailable Coordinator (SPM-47 AC4) is outside this row's AC list and is not asserted.
9. Brief provenance: R9; L37; A3.3. No surviving manual equivalent covers the mid-planning variant; confidence at most Medium while Note 1 is unresolved. Q7 resolved 2026-11-06.
