# E2E-05: Unassigned queue and assignment (SPM-123, SPM-77, SPM-47, SPM-48, SPM-38)

Cross-role end-to-end test (executed in CI on 2026-11-06, PR #128). P0. Covers a new submission entering only the Lead's unassigned queue, the Lead assigning it to coordinator1, the correct person (and nobody else) being notified, and the negatives: another Coordinator cannot view or manage the event, including by direct URL or API, and no Coordinator can take an event from the queue. It crosses Organiser, Lead and two Coordinators and the assignment, notification and access features, which is why it is E2E.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. [S1] SPM-168 Q7 resolved 2026-11-06: organiser not notified on assignment or reassignment. SPM-77 AC1 names only the assigned Coordinator; no story notifies the Organiser. The case asserts the absence at step 7 as written. This P0 gate is cleared.
2. [S2] A second assignment of an already-assigned event: no AC states the refusal or its status code. SPM-123 AC5 says the event leaves the queue after assignment and SPM-47 AC7 covers a changed event being refused for reassignment. The case asserts refusal, no change and no notification, and marks the code ASSUMPTION.
3. [S3] No AC states that the Lead cannot approve or reject. It rests on the Established facts in SPM-168 (the assigned Coordinator approves or rejects; the Lead only assigns and reassigns).
4. SPM-38 AC5 ("assignment is done in round robin") is superseded by SPM-123 and is deliberately not asserted. SPM-38 AC4 is live and is asserted.
5. The SPM-123 AC6 negative (assigning to a Coordinator who marked themselves unavailable) and AC7 (no Coordinators available) are not part of this case's brief row and are not asserted. They appear as the lost-suite flow L29, a proposed addition in SPM-168.
6. The ordering assertion (fewest active first, SPM-123 AC4) is derived from the AC wording. Active counts count Submitted, Approved or Confirmed only (SPM-123 AC3); the SPM-148 AC6 statement that Planning also counts is not exercised here because no seeded event is in Planning.
7. SPM-47 AC1's list of statuses omits Planning while SPM-148 AC6 says Planning events appear in the Lead's coordinator list; the discrepancy is relevant to E2E-06, and is only observed here.
8. Existing Confluence draft `E2E-COORD-QUEUE-01` (36438146) covers assignment only; this case complements it by adding the negatives and the exclusion assertions. Brief provenance: R8; L29 (proposed addition), L31, L37, L108; A3.2, A2.2.
9. The exact bell notification text for SPM-77 is not fixed; the case asserts the three SPM-77 AC2 facts (event name, assigned to me, proposed date) and the link, not a sentence. API refusal codes are ASSUMPTION.
