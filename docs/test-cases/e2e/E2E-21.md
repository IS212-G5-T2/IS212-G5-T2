# E2E-21: Register, capacity, withdraw (SPM-139, SPM-61, SPM-62, SPM-63, SPM-99, SPM-120, SPM-138, SPM-135)

Cross-role end-to-end test (DRAFT, not executed). Covers an Attendee registering for a Confirmed event, the Organiser and the assigned Coordinator seeing the registration list, the event filling to its registration capacity, the next Attendee being stopped (and offered the waiting list), a withdrawal releasing a place, a waitlisted Attendee being promoted, and the negative cases (unconfirmed, cancelled, registration-disabled event; internal planning hidden from Attendees). It crosses Attendee, Organiser and Coordinator and the registration, event-status and notification features, so a component or API test cannot stand in for it. Priority: P0 (proposed) for the registering half (steps 1 to 9), P1 for the rest, as the plan and SPM-168 set.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. Conflict C5 (plan versus story). The plan says the next Attendee "is blocked with a clear message". SPM-138 AC1 says that at capacity the Attendee "cannot register directly and I can join the waiting list". The plan wins for expected results (rank 1), but the story is recorded: this case asserts the block on direct registration (no Register control, 422 `registration_full`) AND the waiting-list offer on the page. The two are compatible as written; if the customer wants a plain "full" message with no waiting list, steps 11, 17 and 18 change.
2. SPM-168 Q10 (exact registration success text and the Registered/Withdrawn/Waitlisted wording) is open. This case asserts the status label (Registered, Withdrawn, Waitlisted, Confirmed) and message intent as read from the ACs, not a copied sentence. Each label is an assumption (S10); put the literals in named constants at the top of the spec so a changed answer is a one-line edit. Priority gate: Q10 gates the P0 half; the case may be written now but its text assertions are Blocked by Q10 until the literals are agreed. Max confidence: Medium.
3. Priority split. The plan sets P0 for registering and P1 for the rest. Steps 1 to 9 are the proposed P0 sub-case E2E-21-A; the story owner decides the final suffixes (brief section 7.1).
4. The waiting-list steps (17, 18) go beyond the plan's wording, which has "another can register" after a withdrawal. The brief's row includes SPM-138 AC1 to AC3, AC9 and AC10. AC10 (leave the waiting list before the start time, status Withdrawn) is not exercised here because only three Attendee accounts are seeded and all are in use; it is covered by backend tests (`JOIN-WAITLIST` family) and could be a follow-up step if a fourth Attendee fixture appears. The plan does not mention promotion; it is added because SPM-138 AC8 and AC9 make it the visible effect of the withdrawal in step 18.
5. Q7 does not apply to this journey, but "who is notified" is asserted: the only notification the stories require is the promotion notice (SPM-138 AC9). Registration and withdrawal by an Attendee are not stated to notify the Organiser or Coordinator; step 9 asserts their absence under assumption S5 (nothing is sent). Re-check if the customer says otherwise.
6. Assumption S6: the builder's `capacity` option opens registration an hour before seeding and closes it a day before the event; an event with no capacity has registration off. This is read from the fixture code, not from an AC, and is a fixture fact, not an expected result.
7. Assumption S7: the registration report refusal status for a user who does not manage the event (SPM-63 AC5) is 403; the frontend comment in `App.tsx` says "MSG-08 on refusal". Confirm the status and code from the SPM-63 Confluence matrix before the spec asserts it; until then assert only "refused, no attendee data in the body".
8. No AC states who is notified when an Attendee registers or withdraws; see Note 5.
9. Assumption S9: a registration attempt for a Cancelled event is refused; the exact status is not fixed by any AC. SPM-136 AC7 says registrations of a cancelled event show Cancelled but does not say a new registration is refused. Assert "refused and stores nothing" and take the code from the matrix.
10. "Attendees cannot see internal planning" has no story AC (SPM-168 AC2 and the plan's Week 1 non-functional rule only). Which API reads are "internal planning" is an assumption (S10b): `planning`, `history` and `arrangements-status` of the event. Confirm the list against the access model before asserting specific codes.
11. No builder exists for reading the database. The "database effect" in this case is verified by authenticated API reads (`registrations/me`, the registration report, `/api/notifications`). A read-only `db.query()` helper would be a prerequisite for asserting raw rows; do not assume it exists.
12. Mobile and clock: not relevant. The backend keeps real time; the case does not move the clock.
13. Overlapping Confluence pages: `E2E-REGISTRATION-01` (page 33620135) and `E2E-REG-WITHDRAW-01` (page 36372574). Neither was opened for this case; they are named from the brief. This case complements them (it is the single-journey form of both) and does not supersede them until the story owner decides.
