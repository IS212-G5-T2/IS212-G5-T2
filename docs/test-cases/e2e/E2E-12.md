# E2E-12: Hold converted before expiry (SPM-159, SPM-132, SPM-56, SPM-51, SPM-165, SPM-134)

Cross-role end-to-end test (DRAFT, not executed). Covers an Event Coordinator requesting a tentative hold, Venue Staff approving it (which sets a 336-hour expiry), a second Coordinator being blocked from the held slot, and the hold being converted to a confirmed booking before it expires. It crosses two Event Coordinators and Venue Staff, and it is E2E rather than cheaper because the expiry timestamp, the calendar state, the block on the competing request and each notification are produced by different real endpoints and must agree.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. [S1] BLOCKED by SPM-168 question 8 (does a tentative hold block a competing request outright, or only warn?). The plan says "blocked or warned (per customer rule)". The stories block outright (SPM-56 AC4, SPM-51 AC7, SPM-159 AC7); the case assumes that, and steps 8 to 10 and 15 are the assertions that change if the answer is "warn only". The case may run before the answer arrives on that assumption; the assumption belongs in a named constant at the top of the spec.
2. Plan-versus-story conflict [C2]: the plan has the Event Coordinator "complete the required action before T" so that "the hold becomes a confirmed booking"; SPM-132 AC9 has Venue Staff convert the hold, and SPM-134 AC3 describes the Coordinator being notified when Venue Staff convert it. The plan wins for expected results, but it names no Coordinator action, no story defines one and no control or endpoint was found in the sources read, so step 11 is BLOCKED and the conversion is performed by Venue Staff. Step 11's negative flips if the customer confirms the Coordinator converts.
3. SPM-168 question 4 (reminder lead time) is not in play: the reminder fires 48 hours before expiry (SPM-134 AC1), the expiry here is 336 hours out, and the reminder is not due during the test; the case asserts no reminder is present.
4. The stored form of a converted hold (which status value, and whether the request kind changes) is not given in an AC. The case asserts "no expiry value" and the calendar and Bookings page text, not a column value.
5. Whether a hold request counts as the "first venue booking request" that moves an Approved event to Planning (SPM-148 AC1) is not stated. The case therefore does not assert the event's status after step 3. Open item; a Planning assertion can be added once decided.
6. [S2] Refusal status and error codes for the API steps are not in any AC. Assert 4xx plus unchanged rows; read codes from the route specification, not the service.
7. Expiry arithmetic is asserted as a window around the real database clock (`now` read before and after the approval click), not as an exact instant, because the approval time is real. The expected value is 336 hours (SPM-132 AC6, SPM-159 AC4), which is a story-owner proposal pending customer confirmation (SPM-159's policy note); it is not a customer answer.
8. Builder gaps: no hold-request builder, no booking-request builder; `coordinatorEmail` and event start and end options are proposed and unverified.
9. Confluence overlap: none specific to conversion. This case supersedes nothing.
