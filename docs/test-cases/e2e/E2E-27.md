# E2E-27: Change request declined (SPM-65, SPM-64, SPM-157)

Cross-role end-to-end test (DRAFT, not executed). Covers the Organiser's change request being denied by the assigned Coordinator with a reason, the Organiser learning the outcome and the reason, and every original arrangement (event details, venue booking, registrations) staying exactly as it was. It crosses Organiser and Coordinator (and a second Coordinator who must be refused) and the change-request, history and notification features; the unchanged arrangements can only be proved against the real database.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. SPM-168 Q3 resolved 2026-11-06: 10 to 500 characters, at least 3 real words. The reason satisfies this rule. This case is unaffected.
2. The plan says the Organiser "is notified with the reason". No SPM-65 AC words this. The registered tests REVIEW-CHANGE-05-D and REVIEW-CHANGE-07-D cover the Organiser notice; the wording and whether the reason is in the notice or only on the event page must be confirmed against matrix 28016830, which was not opened. Until then the case asserts that exactly one notice exists for Organiser 1 and that the reason is visible on the event page, without asserting the notice sentence.
3. Assumption S3: a denial needs a non-empty reason (SPM-65 AC5 says "provide a reason" and SPM-55 AC3 requires one for booking rejections). The AC does not say a blank reason is refused. If the product accepts a blank, step 6 is wrong, not the product, until the story owner decides.
4. Refusal statuses for the Coordinator 2 and Organiser 1 attempts are assumptions (SPM-168 AC2 gives only "refused"); confirm from the matrix. SPM-38 AC4 ("I cannot access request details belonging to another Coordinator") and SPM-47 AC6 are the closest ACs for the Coordinator 2 refusal.
5. "All original arrangements are unchanged" is asserted as event details, registration capacity, the venue booking and the registrations. Equipment is not part of this fixture (no equipment-request builder exists); add it if the story owner wants the reservation included, creating it through the product as in E2E-22.
6. The second request in step 12 is the SPM-64 AC8 withdrawal path and SPM-65 AC11. The proposed addition "second change request blocked while one is pending" (SPM-168, proposed additions) is not run here; it is a separate decision.
7. No DB read helper exists; stored state is read through authenticated API reads. The Lead and Venue Staff notification absences are read via `GET /api/notifications` as each user.
8. Overlapping Confluence page: `E2E-CHANGE-REQ-01` (page 33554508) covers a description change request; it was not opened for this case. This case complements it (the denial path with arrangements unchanged) and does not supersede it.
