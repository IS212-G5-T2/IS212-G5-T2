# E2E-11: Confirmed booking blocks a competing request (SPM-56, SPM-55, SPM-54, SPM-51, SPM-137)

Cross-role end-to-end test (DRAFT, not executed). Covers two Event Coordinators who request overlapping slots at one venue, Venue Staff approving the first, and the second request then being unapprovable and, if the Coordinator tries again, unsubmittable. It crosses two Event Coordinators and Venue Staff, and it is E2E rather than cheaper because the block has to hold on the approval-time recheck, on the submission path and on the notification record, each through the real backend.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. Plan-versus-story conflict [C1 family] and open item U5: the plan says EC2's request "now shows as conflicting, VS cannot approve it, and EC2 is told why". The stories differ. SPM-56 AC2 shows the conflict alert to Venue Staff at approval time; SPM-137 AC7 says a conflicting decision creates no notification; SPM-54 AC2 and SPM-51 AC7 tell the Coordinator why only when she tries to submit. No AC tells EC2 at conflict time that her pending request conflicts. The plan wins for expected results, but the plan's "EC2 is told why" cannot be asserted as an in-app conflict message on the pending request without inventing an AC. The case therefore asserts the three supportable channels (steps 7, 8 and 10) and marks "EC2's pending request shows as conflicting and tells her why" as BLOCKED until a story or the customer defines it. Step 7 asserts only that no rejection reason appears.
2. Plan-versus-story conflict [C1]: "flagged before it reaches VS" (plan) against "cannot submit" (SPM-54 AC2). Here the pending request exists only because it was submitted before the first booking was approved; step 8 asserts the story's refusal for a request made afterwards.
3. [S1] Pending requests do not block one another at submission: SPM-56 AC1, AC4 and AC5 name only approved bookings and active holds as blockers, and no AC states the rule for ordinary pending requests (SPM-159 AC8 states it for holds). If the product blocks at submission, step 2 is refused and the approval-time recheck needs a controlled fixture, as SPM-56's note permits.
4. [S2] Refusal status and error codes for the API steps are not in any AC. Assert 4xx and the unchanged rows; read exact codes from the route's specification when writing the spec, not from the service. Which role may decide a booking is from SPM-55's "As a Venue Staff" wording.
5. The overlap is more than two hours, so the case does not depend on SPM-168 question 1 (back-to-back); that boundary is E2E-09.
6. Rejection reason length (SPM-168 question 3) is not under test; the reason satisfies every candidate rule.
7. Builder gap: no booking-request builder; `coordinatorEmail` and event start and end options are proposed and unverified.
8. Confluence overlap: `E2E-VENUE-BOOKING-01` (page 33554524) complements this case; nothing is superseded.
