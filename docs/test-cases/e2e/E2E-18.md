# E2E-18: Safety gate enforced (SPM-129, SPM-130, SPM-131, SPM-135, SPM-128, SPM-148)

Cross-role end-to-end test (executed in CI on 2026-11-06, PR #128). Priority P0. The Safety Officer gate works in both directions: the Safety Officer cannot review an event whose venue and technical arrangements are not confirmed, and the Event Coordinator cannot confirm the event before the Safety Officer approves. Every negative is asserted through the UI and by direct API call, then the positive path runs once. It crosses Coordinator, Venue Staff, Technical Support, Safety Officer and Organiser, and it is mostly negatives.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. SPM-168 Q9 (preparation versus confirmation), answered by the brief and SPM-168 as a mapping rather than a question: there is no `Preparation` status. The implemented statuses are Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Planning, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled. Approved moves to Planning on the first venue request (SPM-148 AC1); Confirm requires approved venue bookings, reserved equipment and Safety Officer approval (SPM-135 AC1/AC2). The plan's "SO cannot review before arrangements are confirmed" and "EC cannot move to preparation or confirmation before SO approval" are therefore asserted as: the event is in Planning, the Safety Officer cannot see it until the arrangements are confirmed, and the Confirm action is refused until the Safety Officer has approved. The case does not assert any status named Preparation. Assumption proceeded on: there is no separate step between safety approval and Confirm other than the Confirm action itself. Q9 resolved 2026-11-06: confirmed as intended — preparation and confirmation are separate steps.
2. The plan's "after approval the event proceeds to preparation" is read as: the event stays Planning and Confirm becomes allowed. It is not asserted as a status change at approval.
3. The readiness conditions are written from the ACs (SPM-135 AC1, SPM-129 AC1); the API error codes (EVENT_NOT_CONFIRMABLE, 422) are not fixed by an AC and are ASSUMPTION. The case must not copy them from the implementation if the story owner prefers different codes.
4. SPM-129 AC1 does not say what "technical arrangements confirmed" means for an event that needs no equipment. The status controller exposes a "confirm no equipment" action; its use here is not exercised and is an open question for the "ready" setup.
5. The names of the direct URL for a safety event detail page and of the confirm route body are unverified (`POST /api/events/:eventId/status-change` is read from `status.controller.ts`; its body was not checked). Verify before writing the spec.
6. No AC says who is notified when the Safety Officer approves beyond SPM-131 AC1 (the Coordinator) and, on confirm, SPM-135 AC5 (the Organiser). All other absence assertions rest on no story notifying those roles (SPM-168 AC1).
7. Builder gaps: no builders for booking requests, equipment requests or safety checks; `status: "Confirmed"` sets status only. States "early" and "ready" are built through the UI as set-up steps; this is long and may be moved to API helper calls only for set-up (the assertions remain real-stack).
8. Existing Confluence pages: none for the Safety Officer. Provenance: R25, R26; L79, L77 (brief). Complements `E2E-EVENT-CONFIRM-01` (36503689) which covers confirmation only.
