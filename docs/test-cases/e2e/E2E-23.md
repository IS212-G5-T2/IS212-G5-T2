# E2E-23: Attendance beyond venue capacity (SPM-64, SPM-65, SPM-85, SPM-54, SPM-52, SPM-55, SPM-129, SPM-139)

Cross-role end-to-end test (executed in CI on 2026-11-06, PR #128). The Week 1 example, end to end: a Confirmed event for 80 at a 100-seat venue, the Organiser asks for 150, the Coordinator reviews and approves, the booked venue is shown to be unsuitable, the Coordinator books a 200-seat venue, Venue Staff approve it, the safety check is repeated if required, the registration capacity is raised, and the Organiser is told. It crosses Organiser, Coordinator, Venue Staff, Safety Officer and (for the registration half) Attendee, and the change-request, suitability, booking, safety-check and registration features. It is blocked in part on SPM-168 Q6 (does a post-confirmation attendance change need a new safety check?), see Notes 1.

**Notes (open questions)**

1. SPM-168 Q6 resolved 2026-11-06: no re-check required for attendance, date or venue changes. Steps 6, 13 and 14 are unblocked and proceed on the assumption that safety check is not reset by those changes. Confidence is Medium.
2. [U8] No AC ties an attendance change to the registration capacity; SPM-139 AC3 sets capacity separately. Assumption S2: approving the change leaves the registration capacity at 80 and the Coordinator raises it explicitly (step 16). The plan's "registration capacity updated" is read as that action. If the product should update it automatically, step 15 flips to "capacity is 150 after step 5".
3. The plan says "the venue is flagged unsuitable". SPM-54 AC1 lists warnings for facilities, accessibility and layout, not capacity. The case asserts the flag through SPM-85 AC2 (the change is flagged because 150 exceeds the booked capacity) and the venue search filter (SPM-52 AC2, AC4), and does not assert a capacity warning at the booking form.
4. The Organiser outcome notice is a plan expectation. No SPM-65 AC words it. The registered tests REVIEW-CHANGE-05-D and REVIEW-CHANGE-07-D cover it; the exact text must come from matrix 28016830, which was not opened. Until then assert that exactly one notice exists for Organiser 1 and none for anyone else, without asserting the sentence.
5. Whether a venue booking request can be made for a Confirmed or awaiting-safety-check event is not fixed by an AC (SPM-54 AC5 covers replacement only when a booked venue becomes unavailable). Assumption S5: the Coordinator can request the larger venue as an additional booking and then cancel the old one (SPM-54 AC7, AC9). If the product refuses it, the journey as written is not runnable and this must become an open question to the customer, as happened for E2E-26.
6. Whether the Hall 100 booking is "returned for review" or stays Approved after step 5 (SPM-65 AC9, SPM-157 AC2) is not fixed by an AC as a stored status; assert only that the page lists it as needing review.
7. Step 11 assumes cancelling the booking needs no extra confirmation beyond the product's usual one; SPM-54 AC10 requires a warning only when no active booking would remain.
8. Assumption S8 (SPM-137 AC1): booking decisions notify the Coordinator only. The Organiser is not a recipient of the booking decision notice, so its absence is asserted.
9. Step 18 is numbered last but is executed between steps 4 and 5, while the request is pending. The refusal status is an assumption (403); confirm from the SPM-65 matrix.
10. No builder exists for change requests, booking requests, safety checks or equipment requests; all but the safety check and readiness rows are created through the product in this journey. No DB read helper exists; stored state is read through authenticated API calls.
11. The Week 1 80 to 150 example is from the plan's Week 1 briefing summary; the Week 1 briefing itself is not in the repository.
